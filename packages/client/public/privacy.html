Privacy policy

Farthest Outpost (Mars Greenhouse) · Module12 · effective 9 October 2026

The short version

The game collects nothing about you. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics SDKs and no tracking. Everything it stores stays on your device.

What is stored on your device

  • Progress and settings: levels cleared, best times, star ratings, renderer and sound choices. Stored in the app's local storage (IndexedDB; on iPhone and Android also the system key-value store, so progress survives the web view clearing its storage).
  • Play records: for each level attempt, the level id, whether you won, lost or quit, the reason, how long it took and how many times you quit. These help us tune difficulty. They are kept in a queue on your device and are not sent anywhere in this version.
  • Downloaded levels: level files are cached so you can play offline.

None of this contains your name, contact details, location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or any device identifier.

What leaves your device

Only requests for the game's own files (the app and level packs) from our web host. Like any web server, the host may keep standard access logs (IP address, time, file requested) for security and operations; we do not combine them with anything else.

Sharing your play records

Settings has an Export play data button that saves your play records to a file. Nothing is shared unless you choose to send that file to us. If you send it, we use it only to balance levels, and we delete it on request.

Deleting your data

Settings → Reset progress erases progress on the device. Uninstalling the app (or clearing the site's data in your browser) removes everything, including play records and cached levels.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If a future version adds optional online features (for example sending play records), this page will change first and the app will ask before anything is sent. Questions or requests: privacy@module12.com.
