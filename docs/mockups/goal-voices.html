Goal lines and KILN's coaching

Draft for review. Nothing here is in the game yet. Each goal can be spoken by two or three voices, each with its own job:

Human: why it matters, natural speechKILN: how, like a coach (the ship's AI, powered by the reactor)Goal: the exact target and numbers

When one speaker gives both the why and the how, the other one stays quiet.

The four examples, revised

Protein

Sela"The three towns along this road haven't tasted fresh meat in a long while. Whatever you can hunt, I'll buy."
KILN"The rock hoppers are on the flats past the rockslide. Run into one to knock it down, then pick up the protein when it falls. Stay away from the spiked hoppers, because they bite."
GoalDeliver 31 protein

Towing pods

KILN"I can see two pods east of camp, and they are getting colder."
KILN"Walk one circle around both pods, and they will both follow you home."
GoalTow 2 pods home in one trip

KILN gives both the why and the how, so no human speaks. One loop catches every pod inside it, so KILN teaches the quick way.

Holding heat

Tam"I can feel the floor going cold, Boss. That ore pile by the wreck should keep the reactor going a while."
GoalKeep heat at 70% or more for 1:25

Tam gives the why and the where, so KILN stays quiet.

Traders

KILN"Traders leave if they wait too long. Keep the shelf stocked, and stand on the clerk pad when they arrive."
GoalNo trader leaves without buying

A trader who waits too long gives up and leaves without buying; it doesn't stop others coming.

Where each voice shows

Story levels 1–5 keep the human and KILN lines the other account scripted. Only their goal lines change, through the goal templates below.

Regular levels assume the player knows the mechanics. The goal line says only the goal. When a level brings in a mechanic the player hasn't met before, KILN speaks once at the start: "To [the goal, in its words], go to [the place] and [do this]." That replaces today's "On this mission" tip list on the intro card. Regular levels have no human lines.

What the three reviews changed

Three separate reviewers read every voiced line: one for character voice against the existing dialogue, one for the world and the game's rules, one as a first-time player on a phone.

  • KILN talks in connected sentences. He reasons with "so", "because" and "if", speaks in the first person, reports what his sensors pick up, and calls the player Commander, as he does in the story. No more strings of short orders.
  • Humans speak from their own job and never use game words like pad, tray or hopper.
  • Protein falls; it isn't dropped. "Collect the protein when it falls" everywhere, and the scrap line says raiders leave it behind.
  • Game names corrected: spiked hoppers (not spiny), ore pile (not heap), turrets on the sockets beside each gate (not the road), and hiring costs credits.
  • One loop catches every pod inside it, so KILN teaches circling both pods at once.
  • No water anywhere. Sela's towns are "along this road".
  • Flag for the other account: an existing line in story level 2 (and the screenplay) still has Sela say colony ships land "on the coast". I haven't touched it; that file is theirs.
