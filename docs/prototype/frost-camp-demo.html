Frost Camp

Four short levels. Each one adds a few new mechanics on top of the core loop.

Drag anywhere to walk. You can carry several kinds of items at once, up to 6 of each. Everything else happens by standing next to it: chop, fish, feed the fire, stock the counter, ring up customers at the register, and stand on a pad to spend coins.
